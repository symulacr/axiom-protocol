01 · The problem

AI agents that hold money exist today — but you can't own one, can't safely sell one, and can't verify what it did. Wallets hold keys, not agents. Agents hold secrets that can't change hands. "Autonomous" means "unauditable."

Ownership

An agent is a pile of scripts and a private key. No property right exists. You can't buy it, inherit it, or price it.

Secrets on transfer

Sell the agent and the buyer gets your encrypted data re-keyed to their key — or the sale leaks your secrets. Today: usually leaks, or manual trust.

Runaway risk

"Autonomous trading agent" with no on-chain spend limit means one bad model output drains the funds. No receipts, no recourse.

04 · Trust architecture — secrets that change hands safely

The chain only ever sees hashes and sealed keys — never the decryption key, never the plaintext. Here is the re-key dance that makes agent resale safe:

LEG 1

Seal

The agent's brain is AES-encrypted with a DEK. The DEK is sealed (ECIES) to the verifier's key. Only hashes hit the chain.

LEG 2

Prove

On transfer, the oracle signs an EIP-712 ownership proof — bound to verifier + NFT + recipient, fresh ≤7 days, one-shot nonce.

signer allowlist (V2)
LEG 3

Re-key

The oracle downloads the blob, decrypts, re-encrypts with a NEW DEK sealed to the receiver's key, uploads, then the iNFT transfers.

old proofs swept
LEG 4

Contain

A compromised TEE signer? Revoked in one transaction. A rogue admin? Upgrades and fee drains wait a day. A runaway agent? The daily limit already stopped it.

V2 hardening
