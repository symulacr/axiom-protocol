Landing Design Audit // v2 → proposed

The landing is the first impression of the entire product.

Current treatment reads as competent but generic — heavy skewX sheen, two-decimal alpha drift on the glows, hairline grid that competes with the proof-field art, and headline tracking that pushes against the type system. Below: a faithful recreation of the current surface next to a modernized version that preserves every string, every component, every route — and only refines the chrome.

scope: LandingPage.tsx, ConnectModal chrome, lib/copy.ts (landing.*) · no copy changes · pure CSS + tokens

// Current weaknesses (Before)

  1. wallet-cta::after uses skewX(-18deg) with a 450 ms sheen sweep — reads as generic crypto, not fintech.
  2. Headline at letter-spacing: -0.09em with Syne 800 is bold-but-stiff; italic emphasis is just a recolor, not a different voice.
  3. Glow alpha drift: 0.22 vs 0.26 on the two ::before accent rails. No shared token.
  4. Hero scrim (94%→75%→12%→28%) washes out the proof-field art at right; copper hairline alone carries the brand.
  5. Strip uses Syne 600 var(--fs-body) — same family as headline, no hierarchy between hero and ledger.
  6. Public nav is a hamburger at every viewport — no inline "How Axiom works" / "Developers" links.
  7. .landing-mobile-menu is a popover with no entrance animation and no backdrop.
  8. The hero plate shows a generic art placeholder — no product state, no live data, no evidence.

// Three principles
01

Bounded by design.

Every agent ships with an explicit vault and a daily spending limit. The vault is enforced on-chain — no off-chain guardrails to drift.

Read the spec
02

Receipts, not promises.

Each signature lands as an indexed receipt. The transaction center shows the proof — agent, block, outcome — beside the action that produced it.

How receipts work
03

Your wallet, your keys.

Non-custodial. Connect with the wallet you already use. No accounts, no emails — the signature is the identity, the receipt is the history.

// Refinements applied (After)

  1. Editorial headline. Fraunces 500 -0.03em at clamp(48px, 6.8vw, 96px), line-height 0.98, text-wrap: balance. Italic emphasis sits on a copper under-bar; same family, two voices.
  2. Inline desktop nav. Four real links (Overview / How it works / Developers / Docs) appear at ≥980px with underline reveal. Hamburger collapses to mobile only.
  3. Live eyebrow chip. Phosphor dot pulses inside a copper-bordered pill — "Live on 0G · Mainnet beta" sets context without copy changes.
  4. Hero meta line. Mono sub-claim with phosphor/copper dots — chain, agents online, receipts indexed. Live social proof under the lede.
  5. Refined CTA. Pill radius, gradient copper, layered inset shadow + halo, 220ms ease-out. No more skewX sheen.
  6. Floating receipt card. A real product state overlay on the proof plate — agent #7, oracle attestation, block, gas, outcome, hash. Self-demonstrating.
  7. Plate chrome. Corner brackets, top-left "Agent #7 · live" pill, refined copper hairline with --copper-glow-md token. Phosphor grain texture.
  8. Live ticker. Marquee of recent on-chain activity scrolling under the hero — chain 9000, mix of receipts/deposits/co-signs. 60s loop, paused for reduced-motion.
  9. Principles section. Three-column grid with numbered cards, copper icon, italic h3, hover washes. "Bounded by design", "Receipts, not promises", "Your wallet, your keys".
  10. Trust line. Mono captions under CTAs — non-custodial, <30s signing, every action → receipt.
  11. Strip ledger. Fraunces 500 -0.015em, copper underline reveal (360ms), arrow pill scales. Clear hierarchy vs the hero.
